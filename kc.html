Beginner's Guide

Low-cost, easy options to launch your KC Traders website quickly, even if you are not technical.

🌐 Best Platforms

Choose one of these beginner-friendly builders:

Wix.com

Drag-and-drop, easiest for beginners. Free plan available; ~₹200/mo for custom domain.

WordPress + Hostinger

Most popular worldwide. Hostinger India plans start at ~₹69/mo. More flexible long-term.

Instamojo / GoDaddy

Good India-based support, easy UPI payment integration for orders.

📱 Technical Tips

  • Mobile-first design: 80%+ visitors will come from phones. Test on mobile before launching.
  • Compress images: Use TinyPNG.com to shrink photos — fast loading = better Google ranking.
  • WhatsApp button: Add a floating WhatsApp button on every page for easy contact.
  • SEO headings: Your page title should include "sarson tel Bhind" or "mustard oil Madhya Pradesh".
  • Google Business Profile: List your factory on Google Maps for free — local buyers will find you easily.

📋 Content Checklist

Before launching, make sure you have:

  • ✅ Your real phone & WhatsApp number on every page
  • ✅ Factory address (for trust and Google Maps)
  • ✅ At least 3–5 real photos of your oil / factory / packing
  • ✅ Product names, sizes & approximate prices listed
  • ✅ A simple privacy policy page (required by Google)
  • ✅ FSSAI license number (builds consumer trust)
  • ✅ UPI payment QR code or payment link for orders
